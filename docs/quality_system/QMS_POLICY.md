# ProtonAI - Quality & Safety Policy
Doc ID: QP-001 | Version: 1.0 | Status: EFFECTIVE

## 1. Purpose
ProtonAI designs and maintains AI-based software for proton therapy
planning support. This policy defines non-negotiable commitments to
patients, clinicians and regulators.

## 2. Safety principles
P1. Patient safety overrides every schedule, feature and commercial goal.
P2. No output influences real patient treatment before regulatory
    clearance and prospective clinical validation.
P3. Every safety claim must be traceable to verified evidence in this repo.
P4. The software always declares its uncertainty; silence is a defect.
P5. Any contributor holds Stop-Line Authority: anyone can halt a release
    for safety reasons, without justification burden.

## 3. Regulatory commitment
We design for ISO 13485, ISO 14971, IEC 62304, IEC 62366-1,
IEC 82304-1, ISO/IEC 27001, FDA pre-market cybersecurity guidance
and EU MDR Annex I.

## 4. Intended use (draft)
ProtonAI assists qualified medical physicists and radiation oncologists
in contouring and plan evaluation for proton therapy. It does NOT
replace professional judgment.

## 5. Review cycle
Reviewed at every management review; at least annually.
